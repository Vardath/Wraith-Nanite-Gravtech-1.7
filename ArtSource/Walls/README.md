# Authored wall material sources

These masters were rebuilt from the current WNG family atlas designs for the
12 October 2026 wall pass. They retain the Stargate material language:

- Precursor: pale nanite composite, fine angular plates and restrained cyan field seams.
- Wraith: dense bone-colored chitin ribs, muted plum membrane and recessed green biological slits.
- Goa'uld: dark naquadah armor, antiqued bronze geometry and small amber crystal inlays.

The painting was generated as long architectural wall runs using the existing
family art as reference. Each `wall_run.png` retains a six-cell painted strip
with coping and a front face. `master.png` is its quiet 192-pixel structural cap,
made periodic at the edges. The packer takes painted coping and side faces from
the strip only for exposed contours; connected edges receive no endcaps or
crossbands. The sixteen states are different wall structures, not repeated
decorative square sprites. Transparency belongs to their linked masks and
angled cutouts.
Full-resolution intermediate generations remain outside the runtime texture
tree. No label, scene, floor or concept-sheet graphic is used as a game asset.

Production packing and verification: `Tools/WallArt/README.md`.
